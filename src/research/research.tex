In this paper, we consider a charged quantum dot (QD) with resident electron located in optical microresonator.
Optically active transition is transition between ground state and charged trion with energy $E_t$,
equaling to energy of the fundamental resonator mode $\hbar \omega_0$.
The sistem is excited by laser pump with detuning $\Delta$.
Hameltonian of that sistem in the rotation wave approximation is:

\begin{gather}
    \hat{H} = E_t \left( \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{d}_{\uparrow}
    + \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{d}_{\downarrow} \right)
    + \omega_0 \left( \hat{b}^{\dag}_{+} \hat{b}_{+}
    + \hat{b}^{\dag}_{-} \hat{b}_{-} \right) +
    \\ \nonumber
    + g \left( \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{b}_{+} \hat{a}_{\uparrow}
    + \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{b}_{-} \hat{a}_{\downarrow} \right)
    + g^{*} \left( \hat{d}_{\uparrow} \hat{b}^{\dag}_{+} \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\uparrow}
    + \hat{d}_{\downarrow} \hat{b}^{\dag}_{-} \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \right)
\end{gather}
since the pumping of the system is carried out by a laser pulse with a large number of photons,
the interaction with this part can be described by a classical field:

\begin{gather}
    \hat{H} = \Delta \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{d}_{\uparrow} + \Delta \hat{b}^{\dag}_{+} \hat{b}_{+} + \Omega_R \left( \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{a}_{\uparrow}
    +  \hat{d}_{\uparrow} \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \right) + g \left( \hat{b}_{+} \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{a}_{\uparrow}
    +  \hat{b}^{\dag}_{+} \hat{d}_{\uparrow} \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \right) \\ \nonumber
    + \Delta \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{d}_{\downarrow} + \Delta \hat{b}^{\dag}_{-} \hat{b}_{-} + \Omega_R \left( \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{a}_{\downarrow}
    +  \hat{d}_{\downarrow} \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \right) + g \left( \hat{b}_{-} \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{a}_{\downarrow}
    +  \hat{b}^{\dag}_{-} \hat{d}_{\downarrow} \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \right) \\ \nonumber
\end{gather}
where the optical mode creation operator in circular polarisation $\sigma_{\pm}$: $\hat{b}_{\pm}^\dag = \left( \hat{b}_{+}^\dag \pm \hat{b}_{-}^\dag \right)/\sqrt{2} $;
the electron creation operator $\hat{a}_{\uparrow\downarrow}^\dag$, where $\uparrow\downarrow$ denotes the electron state $\left| \pm 1/2 \right>$;
the trion creation operator $\hat{d}_{\uparrow\downarrow}^\dag$,  where $\uparrow\downarrow$ denotes the negatively charged trion state with heavy hole spin $\left| \pm 3/2 \right>$;
$g$ is the effective QD-cavity coupling parameter; $\Omega_R $ is Rabi frequency.

QD is located in horizontal magnetic field for controlling the spin polarisation of resident electron.
Hameltonian of that intraction is:
\begin{gather}
    \hat{H}_{B} = \mu_B g_e B_x \left( \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{a}_{\uparrow} + \hat{a}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{a}_{\downarrow}  \right) +
    \mu_B g_t B_x \left( \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\downarrow} \hat{d}_{\uparrow} + \hat{d}^{\dag}_{\uparrow} \hat{d}_{\downarrow}  \right)
\end{gather}
where $g_e$ g-factor of electron, $g_t$ g-factor of trion.

In order to describe evolution with the escape of photons into the environment, evolution equation have to be:
\begin{gather}
    \partial_t \hat{\rho} = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \left[ \hat{H} ; \hat{\rho} \right] + \sum_i \gamma_{i} \left( \hat{V}_i^{\dag} \hat{V}_i \hat{\rho} + \hat{\rho} \hat{V}_i^{\dag} \hat{V}_i - 2 \hat{V}_i \hat{\rho} \hat{V}_i^{\dag} \right) \equiv \hat{\mathcal{L}}t
    \label{eq:lindblad}
\end{gather}
where $\gamma_i $ and $\hat{V}_i $ are the rate and jump operator in the Lindbladian.
It includes the photon yield from the cavity mode with $ \gamma_{photon} = \Gamma $, and $\hat{V}_{photon} = \hat{b}_{\pm} $
and phonon pure-dephasing mechanism with $ \gamma_{phonon} = \gamma \Omega^2_R $ and $\hat{V}_{phonon} = \dots $,
and spin relaxation with $ \gamma_{phonon} = \dots $ and $\hat{V}_{spin} = \dots $.
